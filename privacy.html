PRIVACY INFORMATION — DRAFT FOR REVIEW

Website enquiry information

This draft describes how the current enquiry form is intended to work. It must be reviewed and completed before publication.

What you submit

If you complete the enquiry form, we collect your name, work email, organisation, selected area of interest and any details you choose to provide. Please do not include confidential client or employer information.

How it is used

Your information is used to respond to the enquiry and discuss requested training. Enquiries are transmitted by the website’s email delivery provider to the designated Vyratec inbox when configured. Basic operational logs may be maintained by the hosting and email providers. Retention periods and provider details must be confirmed before launch.

Contact

Business contact details and any relevant policy obligations must be confirmed before this notice is published. Return to the Vyratec homepage.
