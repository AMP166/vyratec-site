06 / QUESTIONS

Before we start.

How many people can attend? +

There is no fixed 15-person limit. We scope the training around your audience, venue and desired interaction—from small teams to large rooms. Larger sessions may combine whole-room demonstrations, questions and facilitated group work.

Is this suitable for people who already use AI? +

Yes. We assess the team’s existing capability and avoid spending an advanced audience’s time on elementary prompts. Sessions can focus on analysis, document workflows, verification, agents and practical automation.

Which AI platforms do you cover? +

Training is tool-aware and can be tailored to your current environment. Our existing material includes Google Gemini and Antigravity, alongside concepts relevant to other business AI tools. We agree the software and access requirements in advance.

What about sensitive company information? +

We use synthetic or expressly approved materials and discuss permission boundaries, output verification and human review. We do not need confidential documents in consumer AI tools to teach useful techniques.
