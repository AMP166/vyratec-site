A few practical questions

Planning your training

Our team already uses AI. Is this still relevant?+

Yes. We assess existing skills and focus the session accordingly. Experienced teams can work on more demanding analysis, document intelligence, verification and supervised automation.

Can you work with our existing AI tools?+

We can tailor training to ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot or Google Gemini. We agree platforms, account access and exercises during scoping so the session fits your working environment.

How do you accommodate different group sizes?+

Small groups can work closely through hands-on exercises. Departments and larger audiences can combine demonstrations, facilitated practice and discussion. We scope group size, venue and facilitation with you.

How do you handle sensitive information?+

Training uses synthetic or expressly approved materials. We cover your organisation’s data boundaries, suitable tool access and ways to verify outputs before using them in business decisions.

Can AI run within our own environment?+

Yes. Suitable models can run on laptops, workstations or dedicated GPU systems. We help assess the workload, hardware and output requirements, then tailor training to the selected environment. Data control also depends on connected tools, integrations, logs and access settings, so we consider the complete workflow.

What happens after the training?+

Your team leaves with reusable methods and an action plan. If you need more support, we can discuss follow-on sessions, office hours and manager check-ins as part of a scoped adoption programme.
